Analytical Constraints & Criteria

  • • Minimum Area: ≥ 20 000 m² (2.0 ha) measured in EPSG:3301 (L-EST97)
  • • Land-use (siht1): Agricultural, Production, or Commercial in Tartu linn (79,056 county parcels evaluated)
  • • Highway Accessibility: ≤ 2 000 m planar distance to primary/secondary highway or planned connector
  • • Education Catchment: ≤ 25 min walk (2,000 m at 4.8 km/h) to public school and kindergarten

Assumptions

  • A1 — "Near a main road" is interpreted as <= 2000 m planar distance to a national primary (Põhimaantee) or secondary (Tugimaantee) road or planned connector.
  • A2 — Parcel area is measured in EPSG:3301 (L-EST97).
  • A3 — "25 minutes walking distance" to municipal schools and kindergartens is modeled as a planar catchment radius of <= 2000 m based on a standard walking speed of 4.8 km/h (80 m/min).

Project Overrides (2)

  • OVERRIDE-001 (modify_attribute) — The source dataset contains a closed facility; marked as inactive.
  • OVERRIDE-002 (add_feature) — The planned connector road does not yet exist in official machine-readable geospatial datasets but connects Tähtvere development zone to the main highway network.
